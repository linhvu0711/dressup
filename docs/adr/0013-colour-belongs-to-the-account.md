# A colour belongs to the account

A colour belongs to the account, not to one kind of clothing. It has a name and a colour code, and any kind of clothing can use it. Everything that has a colour points at the account's colour, so a change to a colour shows everywhere, and the app refuses to delete a colour that is in use. We dropped the copy of the colours that each look kept, because the closet, the wishlist, and the combination filter must agree that two navy things are the same navy. This supersedes 0006.
