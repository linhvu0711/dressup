# Owned items can share a piece and brand

An owned item now has a name, so two items of one piece and one brand can be different things, such as an AIRism tee and a Supima tee, both black and both from Uniqlo. We dropped the rule that allowed one item for each piece and brand. Bought now always adds a new item that keeps the shop link's name, price, and pictures. The old rule caught an add made twice by mistake. But it refused real items, and when Bought added one to an item the user already had, it lost the link's name, price, and pictures.
