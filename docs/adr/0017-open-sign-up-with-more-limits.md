# Open sign-up with more limits

Sign-up stays open to any Google account. Each account holds at most 100 colours, 100 brands, 300 combinations, 500 owned items, 200 wishes, 10 shop links for a wish, 8 pictures for an owned item or a shop link, and 1,000 pictures. The pictures of all accounts stop at 9 GB stored and at 900,000 uploads a month. "Delete my account" and the sign-up switch stay. This supersedes 0008.
