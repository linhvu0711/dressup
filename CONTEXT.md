- Type: one kind of clothing in one length. "Short tee" and "long tee" are two types. The app has 17, and the list is fixed.
- Layer: where a type sits on the body: bottom, inner top, sweater, or jacket. Each type is in exactly one layer.
- Piece: a type in a colour, such as "Navy long button-up".
- Combination: 2 to 4 pieces that match well, at most one for each layer. It has no name: its title is its pieces, top layer first. Two combinations with the same pieces are one combination, and the account keeps it once. An account holds at most 300. A jacket in it is always drawn open.
- Builder: the page where the user picks the pieces of a combination on the mannequin, for a new combination or to change a saved one.
- Combinations page: the wall of saved combinations. The user filters it by pieces, by tags, and by the pieces they own.
- Combination page: one saved combination: the mannequin, the title, and a line for each piece with its colour. From 900 px it sits beside the Combinations page.
- Piece page: the page for one piece. A piece line on a combination opens it. It shows the piece's owned items and shop links as cards, and the combinations that hold the piece.
- Own mark: shows on a piece of a combination when the closet has an owned item of that piece.
- Want mark: shows on a piece of a combination when the wishlist has a wish for that piece. A piece can show both marks.
- Mannequin: the figure drawn by code as SVG. Front view, no face, white shoes. Each account sets its skin and hair colour.
- Account: one Google sign-in. Each account has a private space. No account sees another account's data.
- Colour: a name and a colour code that the account saves once and uses on any kind of clothing. The user manages colours on the Labels page. A new account starts with a starter set, and an account holds at most 100.
- Brand: a name that the account saves once and puts on owned items and shop links. An item can have no brand. The user manages brands on the Labels page. An account holds at most 100.
- Owned item: one thing in the closet. It has a piece, a quantity, and an optional brand, name, and price paid in VND. Two owned items can share a piece and a brand, such as an AIRism tee and a Supima tee, both black and both from Uniqlo. An item with no name goes by its piece's name. An account holds at most 500.
- Picture: a photo that the user or an AI app uploads to an owned item or a shop link. The browser shrinks each photo the user picks to 1 MB or less; an AI app sends 1 MB or less to an upload address. An owned item or a shop link holds at most 8, and an account at most 1,000.
- Picture storage stop: uploads stop for all accounts when the stored pictures reach 9 GB, or when 900,000 uploads happen in one month. It keeps Cloudflare R2 inside its free amount.
- Upload address: a web address that takes the bytes of one picture from an AI app, once, within 15 minutes, with no sign-in.
- Tag: a label the user makes and puts on combinations. The user renames and deletes tags on the Labels page.
- Sign-up switch: one on/off environment variable that stops new sign-ups. People with an account can still sign in.
- Label: a colour, a brand, or a tag. The account makes it once on the Labels page and uses it everywhere.
- Labels page: the page with the lists that the account makes once and uses everywhere.
- Closet: the account's owned items, one card each, newest first. A card opens the item's Item page. The user filters the Closet by type, colour, and brand.
- Item page: the page for one owned item, one shop link, or one wish with no shop link. It shows the pictures, the facts, the actions, and the combinations that hold the piece. A shop link's page also shows the other shop links of its wish.
- Wishlist: the account's shop links, one card each, plus one card for each wish with no shop link. A card opens its Item page. The user filters the Wishlist by type, colour, and brand.
- Wish: one wanted piece, with its shop links. A piece has at most one wish. When the user removes the last shop link of a wish, the wish goes too. An account holds at most 200.
- Shop link: one place to buy a wish: a web address with its own brand, name, and price in VND. A wish holds at most 10.
- Bought: the action that turns one shop link into a new owned item with the link's brand, name, price, and pictures. It deletes the wish and the wish's other shop links.
- AI app: an outside AI assistant, such as Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, or Codex, that acts in an account for its user.
- Connected AI app: an AI app the user allowed on the Allow page. Settings lists each one. Disconnect stops it at once.
- Allow page: the dressup page where the user lets one AI app see and change the data in their account.
- AI action: one thing an AI app does in an account, such as saving a combination or adding a colour.
- AI action limit: 60 AI actions a minute for each account.
